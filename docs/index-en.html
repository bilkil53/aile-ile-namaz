Protecting your personal data

Two separate confirmations are requested before sign-in: you state that you have read the Information Notice, then you give your explicit consent separately.

I have read and understood the KVKK Information Notice.

Read the information notice

I have read the Explicit Consent Text; I give my explicit consent for my prayer and kaza records and my dua and greeting messages to be processed and shown to the people in my family circle.

Read the explicit consent text

The text of the consent boxes in the app. If the text changes, the app asks for consent again.

Data is stored on servers in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany) over encrypted connections; it is not sold and not used for advertising. Only you and the family circle you belong to see your records. Deleting your account withdraws your consent and deletes your data.
