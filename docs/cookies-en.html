Cookie Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026 · Scope: the Aile İle Namaz website and the Aile İle Namaz app

In short: This website uses no cookies, stores nothing in your browser and does no visitor analytics or ad tracking. That is why we do not show a cookie consent banner.

1. What is a cookie?

Cookies are small text files saved in your browser when you visit a website. They can be used to keep you signed in, remember preferences, count visits or show ads. Under Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK) and the Personal Data Protection Authority's Guide on Cookie Practices, non-essential cookies may only be used with explicit consent.

2. Cookies used on this site

TypeUsed?Explanation
Strictly necessary cookiesNoThe site has no sign-in, basket or session.
Preference cookiesNoNo language or theme preference is saved.
Analytics / statisticsNoNo Google Analytics or similar tool.
Advertising / marketingNoNo ad network, pixel or tracking code.
Local storage (localStorage etc.)NoNothing is written to your browser.

3. Third-party links and resources

  • Fonts and icons are loaded from the site's own files; no requests are sent to Google Fonts or any other server.
  • Hosting: The site is published on the servers of a hosting provider. For security and to provide the service, the provider may keep technical logs such as visitors' IP addresses under its own privacy notice. We have no access to these logs.
  • The download link takes you to the server where the app file is hosted. On that page the service's own cookie and privacy policy applies.

4. Cookies in the app

The Aile İle Namaz app uses no cookies. The app keeps its settings and offline prayer times in the phone's own storage; your session is managed through Google sign-in and our server infrastructure. Details are in the Privacy Policy and KVKK Information Notice.

5. Browser settings

You can view, delete or block cookies at any time in your browser settings. Because this site uses no cookies, these settings do not affect it.

6. Changes

If cookies are ever added to the site for analytics or another purpose, this page will be updated first and your consent will be requested on arrival for non-essential cookies.

7. Contact

For questions, use the contact address in the Privacy Policy.
